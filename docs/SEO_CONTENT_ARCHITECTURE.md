# SEO content architecture

## Positioning and audience

Inventman is a **business inventory, sales and profitability operating system** for inventory-based small and growing businesses. Primary audiences are owners, operations leaders, inventory teams, buyers, cashiers and finance staff in retail, supermarkets, hospitality, pharmacies, electronics, wholesale/distribution and multi-branch businesses. Industry pages describe supported use cases; they do not represent specialist product packs or regulatory claims.

## Content rules

- One clear intent, unique title, description and H1 per indexable route.
- Human-readable definitions and direct answers precede detailed workflow copy.
- No keyword stuffing, location-page multiplication, competitor claims, fake proof or unsupported “best” language.
- “Offline” always means the provisioned, time-limited supported POS cash-sale flow; the wider workspace is online-first.
- Financial reporting is management reporting and does not claim statutory accounting, tax filing or certification.
- Pricing and trials come from active public plan data rather than authored marketing numbers.

## Entity and conversion consistency

The entity name is always **Inventman**. The category is always **business inventory, sales and profitability operating system**. The primary conversion is **Start Free Trial**, followed by plan selection, signup, verification, onboarding and workspace entry. “View Pricing,” “Explore Features” and contextual educational links are secondary actions.
